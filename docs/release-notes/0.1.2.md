Fixed: a short freeze when the ball hit a paddle with sound on.
The paddle-hit tone no longer holds up the screen, so a fast Rally stays smooth with sound on.
Nothing else changes: the same tones, settings and controls as 0.1.1.

## 0.1.2

`versionName` 0.1.2, `versionCode` 3, built on light-sdk v0.2.0.

- No freeze on a paddle hit (decision log D32). Each tone started on the frame's own thread and waited
  on the phone's audio service for about 100 ms, so every paddle hit with sound on froze the picture
  for six frames. Tones now start on a thread of their own, and a frame never waits for one. A tone
  that a newer one replaces before it starts is skipped, as a new tone already cut off the old.
- The tones play through the phone's normal mixer, not its low-latency path, which 16 to 257 ms
  beeps do not need.
- Each Match's first `PongEvent` line names the sound choice too: `match seed=<n> difficulty=<name>
  sensitivity=<normal|fast> sound=<on|off>`. The frame benchmark (`mise run bench`) reports the sound
  choice and the frames that missed a vsync, and fails when they are more than 0.5% of the frames.

## Known limits

- The faster Paddle makes every Difficulty easier (decision log D31); the Difficulty table and the
  step curve wait on `spin` lines from real play (decision log D22).
- No APK is attached: Pong is not yet in Light's Tool Library, so sideloaders build it themselves
  (README, "Install").
- The wheel's direction is set by one constant, `WHEEL_UP` in `screen/Keys.kt` (counter-clockwise
  moves up). If it feels backwards on your phone, that constant is the one place to flip it.
