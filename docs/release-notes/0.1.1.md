Smoother motion at speed and a quicker paddle. Spin the wheel faster and each click moves your paddle
further, up to twice as far; a slow turn still moves the same short step as before.
A new Wheel row on the title screen makes every click go half as far again.

## 0.1.1

`versionName` 0.1.1, `versionCode` 2, built on light-sdk v0.2.0.

- Even motion at high speed (decision log D30). Frames used to run 3, 4 or 5 Steps as the 60 Hz frame
  clock jittered, so a fast Ball moved in uneven jumps. Now the Ball and Paddles are drawn blended
  between the last two Steps, so the picture moves evenly whatever the display period.
- A rate-based wheel (decision log D31, the owner's ruling after playing 0.1.0). A Detent moves the
  Paddle target 40 px when the wheel turns 10 times a second or slower, rising evenly to 80 px at 40 a
  second, and no further. A slow turn behaves exactly as in 0.1.0.
- The Wheel row on the title screen, under the sound row: "Wheel normal" or "Wheel fast". Fast moves
  the Paddle 1.5 times as far for every Detent (60 to 120 px). Click or tap it to switch; Pong
  remembers the choice in `settings.txt`, and a file from 0.1.0 opens as Wheel normal. The Title's
  rows are 48 dp apart now (56 dp before), on the pause and Match over panels too, so six rows fit.
- Spin logging for tuning. During play, each spin of the wheel writes one `PongEvent` line when it
  ends: `spin detents=<n> ms=<duration> peak_rate=<Detents a second>`. Each Match's first line now
  names the Wheel setting too: `match seed=<n> difficulty=<name> sensitivity=<normal|fast>`.

## Known limits

- The faster Paddle makes every Difficulty easier. In the simulation (decision log D31), a late,
  misjudging player at 30 Detents a second now wins every Match on Hard, and at 15 a second wins 26 of
  200 on Hard, where it won 1 of 200 in 0.1.0.
- Tuning waits on `spin` lines from real play: the step curve's 10 and 40 a second and the Difficulty
  table (decision log D22) are both to be set from measured wheel rates on the phone.
- No APK is attached: Pong is not yet in Light's Tool Library, so sideloaders build it themselves
  (README, "Install").
- The wheel's direction is set by one constant, `WHEEL_UP` in `screen/Keys.kt` (counter-clockwise
  moves up). If it feels backwards on your phone, that constant is the one place to flip it.
