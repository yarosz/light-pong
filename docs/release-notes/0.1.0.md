Pong against the computer, played with the scroll wheel. Turn it to move your paddle, press it to serve.
Three difficulties, first to 11, and a match that waits, paused, when you leave.
Works offline, with no account, no permissions and nothing tracked.

## 0.1.0, the first release

`versionName` 0.1.0, `versionCode` 1, built on light-sdk v0.2.0.

- The title screen: Easy, Normal and Hard, and a sound row. Pong remembers the difficulty and the sound
  choice in `settings.txt`.
- A Match against the Computer, first to 11 Points with no win by two. The player serves first, then
  the serve alternates.
- The wheel moves the Paddle target 40 px per Detent and the Paddle eases to it. A click serves,
  pauses, or chooses a row. Pong consumes the wheel's keys in every state, even when nothing moves.
  Touch works too: drag to move, tap to serve, tap the pause bars to pause.
- Paused: by a click, by leaving the Tool, or after 60 s with the player's serve untouched. Resume
  holds the Ball for 0.6 s; Menu ends the Match.
- Match over: "You win" or "Computer wins", then Play again or Menu.
- Three square-wave tones for a Paddle hit, a Wall bounce and a Point. No vibration.
- A pure, deterministic core: fixed 240 Hz Steps, swept collisions, seeded Serves and Computer, and
  property tests over 400 seeded Matches.

## Known limits

- Difficulty tuning waits for hand tests on the phone. A simulated player with perfect aim, turning the
  wheel 30 times a second, never loses a Point. At 15 a second it beats Easy and Normal almost every Match and Hard
  almost never (decision log D22). How fast a person turns the wheel decides which Difficulty feels
  right, and that has not been measured yet.
- No APK is attached: Pong is not yet in Light's Tool Library, so sideloaders build it themselves
  (README, "Install"). A Light-signed build will need any dev-signed one uninstalled first.
- The wheel's direction is set by one constant, `WHEEL_UP` in `screen/Keys.kt` (counter-clockwise
  moves up). If it feels backwards on your phone, that constant is the one place to flip it.
